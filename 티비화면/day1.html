The Idea

What is Vibe Coding?

Building software by telling AI what you want —
and checking the result.

"Fully give in to the vibes." — Andrej Karpathy, 2025
(the person who named it)

Old way vs. New way

🧱 Old way

  • Study a language for months
  • Type every line yourself
  • One missing ; breaks everything

🏄 Vibe coding

  • Describe what you want
  • AI writes the code
  • You check, you direct

You still think. AI just types.

How does it work?

The AI read millions of pages
of text and code

Now it is very good at guessing what comes next:

You: make a red button → AI: <button style="background:red">Click</button>

This kind of AI is called an LLM (Large Language Model).

⚠️

But the AI can be wrong

Sometimes it invents things that look right but are wrong.
This is called hallucination.

So YOU always CHECK. 👀

The Loop — remember these 4 words

TELL→ WATCH→ CHECK→ FIX↩
say what you want AI builds it open in browser say what to change

We will use this loop every day, all 5 days.

👑

You are the boss

  • You choose what to build
  • AI does the typing
  • You judge: is it right?

You decide. AI builds.

How to FIX

The FIX formula

what I SEE  +  what I WANT
When I click the button, nothing happens. I want it to show a fun fact. Please fix it.
